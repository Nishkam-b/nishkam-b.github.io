Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version: ClipMitra runs on your own computer. Your videos, transcripts and clips stay on your device. We don't have accounts, we don't use cookies or analytics, and we never receive your videos.

1. Who we are

ClipMitra AI (“ClipMitra”, “we”) is a free, open-source tool made by Nishkam Bhatia. It consists of a website at nishtek.in/clipmitra (the interface) and the ClipMitra engine, which you install on your own Windows computer and which does all the processing.

2. Your videos and clips stay on your computer

  • Videos you upload are copied to the ClipMitra engine running on your own computer, not to us or any server.
  • Transcription (Whisper), highlight selection (Qwen3 via Ollama), face tracking (MediaPipe), captions and rendering (FFmpeg) all run locally.
  • Files are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClipMitra\data and are deleted automatically after 72 hours. You can delete them sooner by deleting that folder, or by choosing to delete your clips when you uninstall.
  • We never see the content of your videos, your transcripts, your clips or your caption edits.

3. What the website collects

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers. The ClipMitra pages don't store anything in your browser.
  • The website is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you load the pages. This is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement; we don't receive or use these logs.
  • The page connects to 127.0.0.1, which is your own computer, to talk to the ClipMitra engine. Your browser asks for permission (“local network access”) before this works.

4. Connections ClipMitra makes from your computer

ClipMitra contacts these third-party services only when needed. We don't control them, and their own privacy policies apply:

  • During setup: Microsoft winget (to install FFmpeg, Ollama, Deno and uv), GitHub (ClipMitra's code), Hugging Face (Whisper speech model), Ollama (Qwen3 model) and Google Cloud Storage (MediaPipe face model). These are downloads; your videos are not sent.
  • When you paste a video link: your computer downloads that video directly from the platform you chose (for example YouTube or Instagram), using the open-source yt-dlp tool. That platform sees the request as coming from your internet connection.
  • Nothing else: the AI models run offline once they are downloaded.

5. Information you choose to send us

If you contact us (for example through GitHub issues or an Instagram message), we only use what you send to reply and to improve ClipMitra. GitHub issues are public, so please don't include private information or attach private videos. If you share your install.log, check it first: it contains file paths on your computer, which may include your Windows user name.

6. Children

ClipMitra is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Your rights

Because we don't collect or store your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Everything ClipMitra stores is on your computer and under your control.

8. Changes to this policy

If ClipMitra starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. The full history of changes is public in the GitHub repository.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub or message @nishkambhatia on Instagram.
