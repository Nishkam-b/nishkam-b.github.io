Frequently asked questions

Everything about installing ClipMitra, keeping your videos private, and getting great clips.

Safety & privacy

Is ClipMitra safe to install?+

Yes. Here is exactly why, so you don't have to take our word for it:

  • It's open source. Every line of code, including the installer, is public on GitHub under the MIT license. Anyone can read what it does.
  • Your videos never leave your computer. All AI processing runs on your own PC. Nothing is uploaded to us or to any cloud AI service.
  • No administrator rights. The installer only writes to your own user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\ClipMitra). It can't change system files.
  • Well-known tools only. It installs FFmpeg, Ollama, Deno and uv through winget, Microsoft's official Windows package manager, and downloads AI models from their official sources (Hugging Face, Ollama, Google MediaPipe).
  • Closed to the outside. The ClipMitra engine only listens on 127.0.0.1, so other devices on your network or the internet can't reach it. It also refuses requests from every website except nishtek.in.
  • No account, no tracking. No sign-up, no cookies, no analytics. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Easy to remove. Uninstall any time from Settings → Apps → ClipMitra AI.
Why does Windows say “Windows protected your PC”?+

Windows shows this for programs that aren't code-signed. Code signing is a paid certificate (₹15,000+ per year) that many small, free projects don't have yet. It doesn't mean the file is harmful: it means Windows doesn't recognise the publisher.

Click More info → Run anyway to continue. If you want to double-check the file first:

  • Download it only from this site or the official GitHub releases page.
  • Right-click the file → Scan with Microsoft Defender.
  • Compare its SHA-256 fingerprint with the one on the release page: open PowerShell and run Get-FileHash ClipMitra-Setup.exe.
My browser asks to let nishtek.in “access apps on this device”. Should I allow it?+

Yes, this is how the page talks to the ClipMitra engine running on your computer. The permission only lets nishtek.in reach apps on your own device; it doesn't give the site access to your files, camera or anything else. If you blocked it by mistake, click the icon at the left of the address bar, set Local network accessto Allow, and reload.

Does ClipMitra upload my videos anywhere?+

No. Uploading a file in ClipMitra copies it from your computer to the ClipMitra engine on the same computer. Transcription, highlight finding, face tracking, captions and rendering all happen locally. Videos and clips are deleted automatically after 72 hours.

Using ClipMitra

Is ClipMitra free?+

Yes, completely free, with no limits, subscriptions or watermarks. It uses free, open-source AI models running on your own hardware, so there are no cloud costs to pass on.

What do I need to run it?+
  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
  • About 8 GB of free disk space (tools and AI models are ~4 GB).
  • Recommended: an NVIDIA graphics card with 6 GB or more memory. Without one ClipMitra still works on the processor, just several times slower.
  • An internet connection for the one-time setup (and for fetching video links).
How do I install it?+

Download ClipMitra-Setup.exe, open it and click Install. Setup downloads what it needs (5–15 minutes) and puts a ClipMitra icon on your Desktop. Double-click that icon whenever you want to use it: it starts the engine and opens this page.

If something goes wrong, the setup writes a log to %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClipMitra\install.log. Running the setup again continues where it stopped.

Can I paste a YouTube or Instagram link?+

Yes. ClipMitra can fetch videos from YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X, Vimeo and direct video links, but only use videos you own or have permission to use. You confirm this with a checkbox before every fetch. Private videos, or ones that need a login, can't be fetched; download them yourself and upload the file.

Why is there a 5-minute limit?+

ClipMitra is in its first version and is tuned for videos up to 5 minutes, so processing stays fast on everyday laptops. Longer videos are on the roadmap.

Does it work for Hindi and Hinglish?+

Yes. Hindi speech is captioned in Roman Hinglish (“itna aasan nahi tha”) by default. If the AI mishears a word, fix it in the clip editor under Words; the timing stays exact.

Are the AI results always correct?+

No AI is perfect. Captions can contain misheard words, and the chosen highlights or titles may not always be what you'd pick. Watch each clip before posting; the editor lets you trim clips, fix words and restyle captions.

How do I update or uninstall?+

To update, download and run the latest setup again: your videos and clips are kept. To uninstall, go to Settings → Apps → ClipMitra AI → Uninstall; it asks whether to delete your clips too.
